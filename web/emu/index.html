THE LIMITS 8 slots · 30 seconds a sample (a longer file keeps its first 30 s) · kept in mono at 22 kHz, a little lo-fi, like an old sampler · up to 24 slices: CHOPS plays them from C3 up, a DRUM KIT slices 1–8 · AS A SYNTH plays the sample once a note (no loop) · names 8 letters · any WAV, MP3, OGG or FLAC your browser reads · they live in this browser (clearing its site data clears them): SAVE puts them in a file, and a song code carries the ones the song plays.

THE SPELUNKR BOXA preview of the standalone groovebox: the same software, laid out like its faceplate. Everything here works.

Left hand: the FX pad and the octave. Above the pad, the readout and the ANO wheel: turn its yellow ring for what the readout names, press the centre for the next card, the arrows on its outer ring scroll and change track (on the tracker they move round the steps), hold the centre and turn for the volume. Right hand: the 15 keys (the bottom nine change with the screen: the glitches with FX KEYS on, the tracker's moves on STEPS, mutes on the MIXER, A–F on PATTERNS, drum pads while recording drums, the cards on a page of cards; tap SHIFT for the second set) and the stick (pitch side to side, mod up). On the box the screen isn't touch: the wheel and the keys drive it. Turn your phone sideways for a bigger box.

EXPORT THE SONG

Rendered from the top, faster than real time. 24-bit, 44.1 kHz. What you play live isn't in it: use RECORD for that.

START A NEW SONG?

Changed your mind afterwards? Hold SAVE on the groovebox to undo it.

SHARE THIS SONG

Your song's code: anyone can type it into CODE in their groovebox.

Anyone who opens this link hears your song in their own groovebox, and can carry on from it.

RECORD WITH A METRONOME?

With the metronome, a bar of clicks counts you in, then the clicks keep time while you record. (Start recording while the music already plays and there's no metronome.)

PLAYLIST

Up to 10 song codes, played back to back like an album. Each one plays its song through once (one that has no song yet plays its pattern 4 times), then the next starts. The song you have open stays as it is. Codes last a month from when they were shared, so an expired one is skipped.
